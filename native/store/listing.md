**Full description** (1,542 of 4,000)

```
A breathing pacer for kids, built with autistic children in mind.

A silly animal rises while you breathe in and sinks while you breathe out. Dots along the top show how many breaths are left, and each finished breath turns into a star. At the end the animal smiles, a few stars pop out, and that's it. No points, no streaks, nothing to unlock.

FOR KIDS
• Six animals to pick from: frog, cat, puffer fish, penguin, bunny and a one-eyed monster
• Each animal makes its own noise, plus a slide whistle with each breath
• A soft vibration as each breath starts, so it works with the sound off
• Rainbow colors that change with every breath
• Nothing to read: the child just watches and breathes

FOR GROWN-UPS
• Set the pace by age, or slide it slower or faster
• Count in breaths (3 to 30), not minutes
• Up and down, a figure of eight, or trace a hand
• Press and hold mode: the child sets their own speed
• A quiet mode: one plain circle, calm colors, no sound, no stars
• A first-then strip to show what comes next
• Share a setup with another device using a link

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no in-app purchases. The app has no internet permission at all. Settings and any photo you add stay on the phone.

GOOD TO KNOW
This is a calming activity, not a medical device or a treatment. Small, gentle breaths are enough. Stop if your child feels dizzy, and ask a clinician first if they have asthma or a heart condition.

Also free on the web at breathe.northidaholabs.com.

Made by North Idaho Labs in Moscow, Idaho.
```

## Store settings

- **App or game:** App. **Free.** (Google Play never lets a free app become paid later.)
- **Category:** Health & Fitness. Parenting or Education would also fit.
- **Contact email:** required and shown publicly. Use a project address, not a personal one.
- **Website:** https://breathe.northidaholabs.com
- **Privacy policy:** https://breathe.northidaholabs.com/privacy.html

## Policy forms

- **App access:** everything works without signing in.
- **Ads:** no ads.
- **Content rating questionnaire:** choose "All other app types." Answer no to violence, fear, sex, language, drugs, gambling, user-to-user contact, location sharing and purchases. This should come out as Everyone / PEGI 3.
- **Target audience:** 5 and under, 6 to 8, 9 to 12. Choosing child ages puts the app under Google's Families policy. It already meets it: no ads, no data collected, no outside links in the child's screen.
- **Data safety:** "Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?" **No.** No account, so no deletion request page is needed.
- **Health apps declaration:** pick the option closest to relaxation, stress or mindfulness. Do not claim it treats or diagnoses anything.
- **News, government, financial, COVID:** no to all.

## Before production

- A new personal developer account must run a closed test with at least 12 testers opted in for 14 days in a row before it can publish to everyone. An organization account (needs the North Idaho Labs D-U-N-S number) skips this.
- Upload the signed bundle: `native/android/app/build/outputs/bundle/release/app-release.aab`. Let Google manage the app signing key (Play App Signing); our key is the upload key.
