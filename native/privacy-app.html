What this app does with data

Breathing Pacer for Kids collects no personal information from children or adults. This page is about the Android app only. It says what does and does not happen, for schools and districts that need it in writing.

Last updated28 November 2026

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, names, email addresses, logins, forms or student records. The app does not know who is using it or how often.

Network

The app has no internet permission. It cannot send anything off the phone. It does not contact a server.

What stays on the phone

These notes are about the Breathing Pacer for Kids app on this phone.

SettingsPace, number of breaths, shape, colours, sound and movement choices. Saved on this phone only. Never sent to us. Clearing the app's data removes them.
Own pictureOptional. If an adult chooses a picture for the shape, a small cropped copy is saved on this phone only. It is never uploaded. "Remove the picture" deletes it.
Session countOptional and off unless an adult turns it on. A count of finished sessions per day, for the last 60 days, saved on this phone only. No names, no times of day. "Clear the record" deletes it.

Cloud backup is turned off. Android does not copy these to a Google account.

Payments

The app has no support button and no payment button. It does not ask for money. Nothing in the app is for sale.

Sound and vibration

The app uses vibration only, as a soft tap when a breath starts. Sound, if it is on, is made on the device. It does not use the microphone, camera or location.

The tone is made by the device itself. The optional "In, Out" voice uses only voices already stored on the device, so no words are sent to a speech service.

Shared setup links

An adult can copy a link that carries the settings to another device. The link holds settings only, no names. The app does not send the link anywhere.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, usage tracking, advertising or profiling.
  • No content loaded from other companies: no outside fonts, scripts, videos or social media.
  • No microphone, camera or location access.
  • No notifications.
  • No links on the child's screen that leave the app.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because the app collects none.

Changes

If the app ever starts to collect anything, this page will say so first, with the date.
