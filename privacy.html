What this site does with data

The breathing pacer collects no personal information from children or adults. This page says exactly what does and does not happen, for schools and districts that need it in writing.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, names, email addresses, logins, forms or student records. The site does not know who is using it or how often.

What is stored, and where

SettingsPace, number of breaths, shape, colours, sound and movement choices. Saved in the browser's local storage on that device only. Never sent to us. Clearing the browser's site data removes them.
Own pictureOptional. If an adult chooses a picture for the shape, a small cropped copy is saved in the browser's local storage on that device only. It is never uploaded and is not included in shared links. "Remove the picture" deletes it.
Session countOptional and off unless an adult turns it on. A count of finished sessions per day, for the last 60 days, saved in the browser's local storage on that device only. No names, no times of day. "Clear the record" deletes it.
Offline copyThe browser keeps a copy of the site's own files so it works without a network. It holds no information about the user.
CookiesNone.

What the site does not do

  • No analytics, usage tracking, advertising or profiling.
  • No content loaded from other companies: no outside fonts, scripts, videos or social media.
  • No microphone, camera, location or sensor access.
  • No notifications, purchases or links that lead children off the site.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because there is none to share.

Sound and voice

The tone is made by the device itself. The optional "In, Out" voice uses only voices already stored on the device, so no words are sent to a speech service.

Shared setup links

An adult can copy a link that carries the settings above to another device. The settings sit in the part of the address after the # sign, which browsers do not send to the web server. The link contains no names.

Web server logs

Like every website, the hosting provider's servers briefly record the internet address and browser type of each request in order to deliver pages and keep the service secure. We do not use these logs to identify or follow anyone.

Changes

If the site ever starts to collect anything, this page will say so first, with the date.
