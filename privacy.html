What this site and app do with data

The breathing pacer collects no personal information from children or adults. This page covers the website and the Android app. It says what does and does not happen, for schools and districts that need it in writing.

Last updated27 November 2026

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, names, email addresses, logins, forms or student records. The website and the app do not know who is using them or how often.

What the website stores, and where

These notes are about breathe.northidaholabs.com, opened in a browser.

SettingsPace, number of breaths, shape, colours, sound and movement choices. Saved in the browser's local storage on that device only. Never sent to us. Clearing the browser's site data removes them.
Own pictureOptional. If an adult chooses a picture for the shape, a small cropped copy is saved in the browser's local storage on that device only. It is never uploaded and is not included in shared links. "Remove the picture" deletes it.
Session countOptional and off unless an adult turns it on. A count of finished sessions per day, for the last 60 days, saved in the browser's local storage on that device only. No names, no times of day. "Clear the record" deletes it.
Offline copyThe browser keeps a copy of the site's own files so it works without a network. It holds no information about the user.
CookiesNone.

Optional support on the website

A grown-up may choose to support North Idaho Labs, LLC. The link is on the Grown-ups panel of the website only. It is not on the child's screen.

The link opens Stripe's hosted checkout page. Stripe processes the payment under Stripe's privacy policy.

The tip is voluntary. It unlocks no features. It is not a tax-deductible charitable contribution.

The Android app

The Android app is called Breathing Pacer for Kids. The package name is com.northidaholabs.breathe.

The app collects no data. It has no internet permission. It cannot send anything off the phone.

Settings and any chosen photo stay only on the device. Cloud backup is turned off. Android does not copy them to a Google account.

The app uses vibration only, as a soft tap when a breath starts. Sound, if it is on, is made on the device. It does not use the microphone, camera or location.

The app has no support button and no payment button.

What the website and the app do not do

  • No analytics, usage tracking, advertising or profiling.
  • No content loaded from other companies on these pages: no outside fonts, scripts, videos or social media.
  • No microphone, camera or location access.
  • No notifications.
  • No links on the child's screen that leave the site or the app.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because the pacer collects none.

Sound and voice

The tone is made by the device itself. The optional "In, Out" voice uses only voices already stored on the device, so no words are sent to a speech service.

Shared setup links

An adult can copy a link that carries the settings above to another device. The settings sit in the part of the address after the # sign, which browsers do not send to the web server. The link contains no names.

Web server logs

Like every website, the hosting provider's servers briefly record the internet address and browser type of each request in order to deliver pages and keep the service secure. We do not use these logs to identify or follow anyone. The Android app does not contact these servers. It has no internet permission.

Changes

If the website or the app ever starts to collect anything, this page will say so first, with the date.
